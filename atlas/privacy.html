Privacy policy

How Atlas handles information.

Effective October 17, 2026

This policy describes Atlas OpenClaw, a personal assistant operated by Muhammed Furkan Gök. It covers the assistant’s authorized Google integrations and related processing, as distinct from this informational website.

Information accessed

Depending on the account’s granted permissions and the requested task, Atlas accesses account identity, Gmail message metadata and content, calendar lists and event details, and Google Drive file metadata and content. It also handles personal tasks, plans, reports and operational records. Each account is selected explicitly; authorizing one account does not authorize another.

Google authentication takes place through Google OAuth. Atlas does not request Google passwords. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored using the operator’s macOS keychain. The current Gmail and Calendar integrations use read-only permissions. Drive permissions allow authorized reading and, for the main account, saving app-managed files.

Why information is used

Information is used to fulfill the operator’s requests: summarize important email, prepare plans, track tasks, search documents and save reports. Scheduled summaries use the same purposes. Atlas does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or use it to develop general-purpose AI models. Its use and transfer of Google API data follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Services receiving information

  • Google: provides authentication and the Gmail, Calendar and Drive services. Reports are saved to Drive only as authorized.
  • Anthropic: relevant source content or metadata may be included in requests to Claude to produce summaries and plans. Processing is governed by the operator’s applicable Anthropic service terms and settings; this policy does not promise a particular provider retention period.
  • WhatsApp: requested or scheduled summaries sent through the connected account are processed by WhatsApp and stored in the relevant conversation.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts these informational pages and may process normal website request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. This site does not display private Google data or contain app-added analytics or advertising trackers.

Data transfers are limited to the features the operator authorizes. Work or school content is not automatically copied into a personal Drive. Additional recipients, cross-account transfers and file sharing require explicit authorization.

Storage and retention

Task files, reports, conversation histories, logs and backups may be stored on the operator’s computer. Drive reports and WhatsApp messages remain in those services until removed according to their controls. There is currently no automatic fixed deletion period for local files or backups. Removing an active record may leave a backup copy until that backup is also removed.

Access controls and keychain storage help protect credentials, but the assistant currently runs with host-computer access rather than complete operating-system sandbox isolation. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Choices, revocation and deletion

Google access can be revoked through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized API access but does not automatically delete existing reports, messages, local histories or backups. The operator can disable schedules and remove those records separately. Contact gkkangk@gmail.com to request access clarification or deletion of information handled by Atlas. Third-party services may retain records under their own policies.

Changes and contact

Material changes to data access or use will require updating this policy and obtaining any required consent before the new use. Questions: gkkangk@gmail.com.
