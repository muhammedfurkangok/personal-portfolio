Atlas OpenClaw

A personal workspace, connected with care.

Atlas OpenClaw helps Muhammed Furkan Gök organize personal tasks, prepare daily plans and find information across individually authorized Google accounts.

What Atlas does

  • Gmail: reads authorized messages to identify important items and prepare summaries.
  • Google Calendar: reads calendars and events to support daily and weekly planning.
  • Google Drive: searches and reads authorized files, and saves reports in the main account using access to files created or opened with the app.
  • Tasks: keeps a local task list and delivers requested planning summaries through WhatsApp.

Personal use and account control

This is a privately operated assistant, not a public signup service. Each Google account is authorized separately. Email access is read-only. Sending messages, changing calendars, sharing files and moving data between accounts require separate explicit authorization and appropriate access. Work and school accounts also require compliance with their institutions’ rules.

Where information is processed

Atlas runs through OpenClaw on the operator’s computer. Relevant message, calendar or document information may be sent to Anthropic’s Claude service for AI processing. Requested summaries may be delivered through WhatsApp, and authorized reports may be stored in Google Drive. Local task files, conversation history and backups may retain information.

Read the privacy policy for data handling and revocation details, and the terms of use for usage conditions.
